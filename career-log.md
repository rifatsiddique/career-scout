How to use this file:

- Dump milestones and accomplishments in plain English, the moment they happen.
  Sloppy grammar is fine. No tags, no employer labels, no template.
- Only convention: a `## YYYY-MM-DD` heading per entry.
- Got a correction? Add a new entry starting with `CORRECTION:` — this file stays
  append-only even when the past turns out to be wrong.
- Say `log <text>` in a session to append from the CLI instead of your editor.

Then run `curate` to fold new entries into your CV and story bank.
